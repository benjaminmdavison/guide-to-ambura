Characters

  • Auberon
  • Captain Bovious: master of the Seadream Chaser, with second mates Biggsby, Jonah, and Ishmael.
  • Captain Lars Stone-Singer: Dwarven captain, known to the party.
  • Faldbar the Red: a talented yet unwise mage who took the name of an ancient dragon. He is now on the run after inadverdently awakening the dragon.
  • Falt-bath-ar-zet-ghul-pheng: an ancient red dragon who does not like his name being taken by unwise mages.
  • Gran: the aged and esteemed leader of Bramblewood.
  • Gusmaro Wind-Finder: Harbormaster, St. Alberich.
  • Gunzar: A strange mage who lives in The Crossroads.
  • Lieutenant Biggsby: second mate of the Seadream Chaser and also a monkey.
  • Lucifer Sam: proprietor of Nightshade and Nocturne, in the Free City of Porphyrium.
  • Selm and Josin: Zoong's parents.
  • Solomon New-Dawn: Regent of St. Alberich.
