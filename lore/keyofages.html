The Key of Ages

The Key of Ages was whispered into existence from the raw energy of the universe by Ptah, the God of the Craft, the Great Architect of Creation. Ptah gathered the disparate strands of time and space together and looped them around a lump of iron. Heating the strands and the iron in his forge until they were molten, Ptah blew the Breath of Creation into the flames, infusing the slag with the essence of all that could ever be and forging it into the Key.

Creation is order wrought from chaos. As such, chaos is integral to creation. The Key, an embodiment of creation, is simultaneously an embodiment of chaos. Humming with energy, shimmering with otherworldly light, the Key operates with the unknowable logic of the gods. It remembers all that has ever been and all that could ever be; it is in and of everything.

The Key of Ages can shift space and time. It can open portals between planes and roam the endless timelines of creation. It can raise kings and destroy empires. The Key can move mountains and drain oceans, create suns and collapse stars. Ptah's Key is capable of warping and blurring every boundary of existence. It can unravel time, open new dimensions, and shift reality.

Nobody knows exactly why Ptah forged the Key. Some believe he used it to walk the planes of creation and pull together the strands of the world in his continuous creation. Others believe he made it so he could keep his creation close to his heart. Chaotes believe that Ptah made the Key as an homage to Eris, the Goddess of Chaos, with whom he has enjoyed an eons-long on-and-off love affair.

Some believe that Ptah made the Key just to give mortals something to do.

Regardless, the Key is no inert artifact. It is an agent of Ptah. It has will, and it has power. It whispers to those it deems worthy, or perhaps to those it deems useful. Apart from Ptah himself, all who have wielded the Key have discovered that it is the Key who wields them. The only exception to this is the Subboreal Dwarves, whose mastery of craftsmanship so impressed Ptah that he entrusted the Key to them after he finished Creation.

Description and Properties

The Key of Ages resembles a tuning fork. The metal shimmers with strange colors and radiates unnatural lights. The Key seems to change in balance and temperature at random; at times it is lighter than a feather, at other times heavier than lead. It goes from steaming hot to untouchably cold with no warning. Those near the Key sometimes have visions of other worlds or other times. It projects an aura of raw, untamed power and it is absolutely unpredictable.

When wielded by a properly attuned user, the Key opens a dimension door or a portal to another plane, physical location, and/or time, depending on the user's intent; or, the user can attempt to use the Key to substantially alter the material world around them.

However, becoming properly attuned requires godlike powers. Gods and demigods can use the Key without incident; highly skilled magic users have adequate odds of success; untrained lay people are likely to become pawns of chaos.
