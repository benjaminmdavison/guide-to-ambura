The Dragonback Mountains

The ominously named Dragonback Mountains are named for the high peaks and steep drops that resemble the bony back of a dragon. According to legends, in the days before [[The Calamity]] the [[Ancient Dragons]] ruled this imposing range; however, Dragons have not been seen since the days of Brelas Wyrmbane, eons ago. The Dragonback Mountains run east-to-west, beginning on the east coast of Ambura and running across the land, shrinking as they run west and become the rolling hills of the Sea of Grass. Notably, the east-west orientation of the Dragonback Mountains provides a natural boundary which separates the Kingdom of Breland to the north from the Principality of St. Alberich to the south. The South Face of the Dragonback Mountains enjoy pleasant conditions. The sea breeze runs north off the [[Pearl Sea]] and up the mountain face, pushing condensation and heavy weather across the peaks. This makes the North Face of the mountains much more hazardous. A perpetual fog, fast-rolling clouds, and frequent rain and snow give the North Face a much more fearsome reputation. The rain carves steep channels through the granite, loosening boulders and rocks that occasionally crash down the slopes, stirring avalanches in winter and mudslides in summer.  These hazardous conditions, however, have given birth to one of Ambura's most beautiful places: the [[Golden Forest]]. The mineral-rich runoff from the mountains feeds lush and fertile soil, from which springs a sprawling, ancient, old-growth forest. The Dragonbacks are full of hunting trails and caves. The most notable trail is The Narrows, a long and winding trade road that crosses over the Dragonbacks through a notch known as The Narrow Pass. This road connects the Kingdom of Breland with St. Alberich. It is a difficult route, and many travelers and caravans have simply vanished while trying to cross the pass.
