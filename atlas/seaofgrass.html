The Sea of Grass

Located to the west of the Free City of Porphyrium and the River Twain, the Sea of Grass is a land of gently rolling hills covered in thick, lush, tall grasses. There are occasional ponds and lakes scattered between the hills, and a few forested outcroppings.

The tall grasses of the Sea of Grass vary in type. Wild wheat and rye grow tall, as do various types of sawgrass. In some areas, the grasses can grow upwards of six feet high, making navigation through the Sea of Grass quite treacherous.

While a grassland might not seem dangerous, the Sea of Grass is stalked by a variety of dangers. Wildcats, snakes, hyenas, large scorpions, and enormous eagles stalk the grasslands. Local lore holds that there are monstrous Grass Stalkers who wander the brush, seizing and consuming the flesh and the souls of those unfortunate enough to get lost in the stalks. Patches of Quickgrass conceal natural sinkholes in the earth, and the grass itself makes navigation very difficult. Thus, the Sea of Grass is rarely traversed.

The only known settlement in the Sea of Grass is Bramblewood, a thriving Halfling village. The Halflings are skilled survivors in the grasslands, and those who need to pass through the grasslands often pay the Halflings to act as guides.
