Campaign Notes

Session 7: August 5, 2026

The party wandered about town, visited the Temple of Ptah and the Gardens of Eris, and attended a fancy dinner at Saint's Rest, the castle in Saint Alberich. After-dinner cordials transported the party to the realm of Nocturne, ruled over by Auberon, the dreaded King of the Fae. Despite his fearsome reputation he seemed quite pleasant.
The party was informed that they are seeking the Key of Ages, an artifact that was wielded during the days of creation to shape the spheres of creation. The Key is also being chased by a devil named Sabnach the Grand, Prince of the Seven Hells. Sabnach and Auberon have some sort of rivalry that is not clear to the party.
Sabnach's machinations for the Key involve royal intrigue affecting the nearby kingdom of Breland. There, the heir to the throne, Henri Wyrmbane, has gone missing at sea. The King, in his grief, has offered to give Sabnach 'anything he wants' in exchange for the return of his son. Sabnach is using this situation to control the armies and navy of Breland in his quest for the Key, which he will allegedly use to rescue the Prince from...whereever he is.
According to Auberon, Sabnach obtaining the Key of Ages will lead to an age of unchecked chaos, war, and despair. The party must bring him the Key before Sabnach can get it, after which Auberon will do...something...to the key that will prevent Sabnach from exercising his evil plan.
To find the Key of Ages, the party must explore the inhospitable Dragonback Mountains and look for Dragon-Skull Cave. There, they will find their way into the depths of the earth, looking for the Subboreal Dwarves, who apparently have been entrusted with the Key for reasons not explained by anyone thus far.

Session 6: July 30, 2026

The party is aboard the Seadream Chaser and traveling to St. Alberich. After dodging a reef, they sailed through the night, but were then intercepted by a sloop flying a yellow flag with a white lion on it. The sloop, named the Lady Veualiah, rammed the Seadream Chaser. A mage and a handful of marauders attempted to board, but were repulsed by the crew before the Lady Veualiah was rammed by a whale and sunk.

After all that wild seafaring, the party made it safely into port. As a token of his gratitude,Captain Bovious offered the party free passage on the Seadream Chaser for any future needs.
Once ashore, the party met and befriended Gusmaro Wind-Finder, the harbormaster. After a pleasant lunch, they journeyed into St. Alberich and took their rooms at the Windgate Inn, a rather fancy inn at which their accomodation has been paid for by Solomon New-Dawn, the regent of St. Alberich.

Session 5: June 26, 2026

Zoong and Heingveir are aboard the Seadream Chaser. They encountered a band of Kuo-Tua as the ship navigated through the river delta and approached the South Sea.
Later, they befriend the ship's master, Captain Bovious, and his pet monkey, Biggsby.
The ship is attacked by a smaller vessel carrying a crew of about 25 primate pirates: monkeys who have been coerced into villainy by some strange force. The attacking vessel was repelled, and many of the primate pirates were rescued, but the ship was damaged. Zoong helped to repair the ship's rudder, and not a moment too late: the ship barely skimmed the corner of a coral reef that could have caused even bigger problems.

Session 4: June 16-17, 2026

Zoong and Heingveir ventured into The Crossroads and delivered their package to Gunzar. It turns out to be a smuggled spell tome. The party rests at a local inn, only to be caught up in some kind of civil unrest. Imps and a devil were spotted among the affray.
On the journey home, a dragon was spotted in the sky. The city of Porphyrium is abuzz with the news of dragons and the chaos at The Crossroads. The party learned about the lore of the ancient dragons, and learned that a local mad wizard named Faldbar the Red may be responsible for the return of Falt-bath-ar-zet-ghul-pheng, a dreaded Ancient dragon. The party then boarded the Seadream Chaser to journey to St. Alberich.

Session 3: June 10, 2026

Zoong and Heingveir are breaking camp on the road to The Crossroads. They are delivering a bundle of dried herbs and mushrooms to a man named Gunzar, who can be found in the College Row neighborhood in The Crossroads.
