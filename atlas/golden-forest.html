The Golden Forest

The Golden Forest is a thick, beautiful, and haunting old-growth forest that separates the [[Dragonback Mountains]] from [[The Screelands]]. The ancient, towering trees of the Golden Forest sometimes measure hundreds of feet in both height and circumference. Rays of sunlight trickle through the canopy, shining down through the leaves and vines, illuminating new growth rising from the floor of the forest. The haunting and beautiful effect of the sunlight falling through the canopy lends the forest it's name. The trees are not the only denizens of the forest. Endless varieties of flora grow here. Rare and beautiful flowers bloom in the trickling sun; precious herbs sprout from the forest floor. Massive ferns and pungent pitcher plants sprout from the litter. Strange and enchanting mushrooms fruit from the wood itself, some of them delicious, some of them poisonous. Fauna of every variety roam the woods. Herds of elk and deer roam the edges of the forest. Foxes, hares, and small rodents eke out a living. Enormous brown bears forage for berries and hunt small game. Wildcats lurk in the shadows and among the branches, spotted only by the fortunate. While the forest is beautiful, it is not necessarily welcoming. If you must enter the forest, it is best not to go too deep, and to bring only the best of intentions. While her treasures are tempting, the Golden Forest does not yield them lightly. Hunting parties frequently vanish without a trace. Lumber-men are occasionally found brutally killed by their own axes, limbs removed, bones shattered. Careless foragers emerge from the woods as madmen or die choking on their blood and vomit. Bandits looking for hideouts find their lairs, and sometimes their armor, filled with unstoppable armies of ants. The Golden Forest is rather selective about her visitors, and does not hesitate to show her displeasure. Deep in the Forest, shadows move among the high branches in the canopy. There are rumors that treants roam the forest, tending to their kin. Lights flicker in the underbrush, and visitors often hear otherworldly whispers in the breeze. Strange elves appear from nowhere and vanish as suddenly, offering aid or hostility, seemingly at random.  Despite these challenges, or perhaps because of them, Hyacinth Wyrmbane the Beautiful has constructed an outpost on the edge of the forest. Known as [[Woodridge]], it is a serene and tranquil place which seems to enjoy the blessings of the forest. The structure seems to grow from the earth itself, with live trees and vines composing parts of the building. Colorful birds flit about in the courtyard, and fragrant flowers blossom around the base of the building. Here, Hyacinth entertains royal guests and offers hospitality to wanderers. The sickly and the mad are often sent to Woodridge to take in the healing properties of the forest; the results of this treatment are...mixed. Those who believe Hyacinth to be vulnerable in the forest have been proven wrong. Woodridge is protected by a contingent of soldiers. It is rumored that it is also protected by an unseen force. Hyacinth is beautiful and kind, but there are whispers that those who try to take advantage of her or harm her suffer fates worse than death. The Princess wanders the forest freely, listening carefully as it whispers something into her ears. Those attuned to a certain kind of magic can feel her whispering back.
